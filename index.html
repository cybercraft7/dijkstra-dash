How a run works

  1. Pick a materialCream, sugar, or dry ice. The chicken hauls that load.
  2. Set both endsMove the source and the destination, then preview Dijkstra or A*.
  3. Steer the chickenArrow keys follow the line. Leaving it costs a penalty of 3, and the route is drawn again.
